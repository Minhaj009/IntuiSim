# 1D Quantum Harmonic Oscillator: Coherent State Evolution

## Scene 1: Coherent Wavepacket Dynamics
At the quantum frontier, a particle trapped within a parabolic harmonic potential well does not remain statically localized.
Instead, its state can be described as a coherent wavepacket—a minimum-uncertainty Gaussian envelope that oscillates without dispersion.
As the probability density evolves across time, the expected position sweeps sinusoidally between classical turning points, exactly recovering macroscopic harmonic motion.
